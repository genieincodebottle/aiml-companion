# Model Card: Support Ticket Triage

## Purpose

Teaching artifact for the Naive Bayes independence assumption, the four
multiclass strategies, calibration, and threshold-based routing under class
imbalance. **Not a production triage model and not fit for routing real support
traffic.**

## Data

Synthetic. Generated by `src/support_ticket_triage/data/generate.py` from a
documented process with a known dependence structure. No real tickets, no
customer text, no personal data.

The inbox deliberately contains: six categories with a 3% rare class, six
planted redundancy families (`DEPENDENT_CLUSTERS`) that break conditional
independence at a configurable strength, boilerplate tokens carrying zero class
signal, and a small amount of cross-class token leakage.

`dependency_strength` is the experimental dial. At 0.0 the independence
assumption holds exactly and Naive Bayes is the correct model for the data; at
0.95 each family of five tokens carries one signal.

## Models

| model | what it is | why it is here |
|---|---|---|
| `native_nb` | MultinomialNB, multiclass without a wrapper | the subject of the investigation |
| `ovr_nb` | one-vs-rest, 6 binary models | the strategy most people default to |
| `ovo_nb` | one-vs-one, 15 binary models | the cost and the votes-are-not-probabilities lesson |
| `softmax_lr` | multinomial logistic regression | **the control**: makes no independence assumption |
| `calibrated_nb` | MultinomialNB + cross-validated isotonic | the repair |

## Measured results (held out, 2,250 tickets, dependency_strength 0.85)

| model | accuracy | macro F1 | rare-class recall | ECE | sub-models |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| native_nb | 0.9160 | 0.9034 | 0.7794 | 0.0096 | 1 |
| ovr_nb | 0.9160 | 0.9035 | 0.7794 | 0.0122 | 6 |
| ovo_nb | 0.9160 | 0.9034 | 0.7794 | 0.5824 | 15 |
| softmax_lr | 0.9240 | 0.9120 | 0.8088 | 0.0171 | 1 |

Cost of the independence assumption, measured as the softmax-minus-NB accuracy
gap across the dependency sweep: **0.0023 at lift 1.00, rising to 0.0231 at lift
1.87**. Both models lose 6.4 points across the same sweep, which is the data
carrying less information rather than either model failing.

Calibration: ECE 0.0255 to 0.0097, with 95.4% of top-1 decisions unchanged.
Routing at a 0.80 threshold: errors reaching customers fall from 68 to 27.

## Known limitations

- **The 2.3-point cost figure is specific to this vocabulary and document
  length.** Redundancy compounds with more features per document, so a larger
  corpus would show Naive Bayes giving up more. The method transfers; the
  number does not.
- **`ovo_nb` probabilities are normalised vote counts, not probabilities.** They
  are reported so the ECE can expose them. Do not read that row as a calibrated
  model performing badly; read it as a ranking that was never a probability.
- **Calibration is fitted and measured on the same distribution.** No drift
  monitoring, and a calibrator does not survive a change in category mix.
- **The rare class recall (0.78) is the weakest number in the project** and is
  reported rather than tuned away. Fixing it properly means class weights,
  resampling, or a different threshold per class, none of which are done here.
- **No text preprocessing.** Tokenisation, stemming, negation handling and word
  order are absent by construction, since the input is already a bag of words.
- **No fairness analysis.** The synthetic inbox has no demographic attributes.
  A real triage system routing abuse reports would need one.

## Reproducibility

Fixed seeds throughout. `pytest` (33 tests) asserts that the planted dependence
is present at strength 0.85 and absent at strength 0, that the answer key never
reaches fitting code, that calibration lowers ECE while preserving the ranking,
that the routing curve is monotone, and that the rare class scores below the
headline accuracy.

Two of those tests initially failed because they estimated per-class quantities
from 2,500 tickets, which leaves the 3% class with roughly 75 rows. The fixtures
now size themselves to the smallest cell rather than the total.
