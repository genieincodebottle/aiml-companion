# Risk Review: Battery Supply Concentration

Review reference: RR-2026-03
Owner: Head of Supply Risk

## Question put to this review

The LI-18650 battery pack is formally dual sourced and therefore compliant with
NWI-SC-004. The supply board asked whether that compliance corresponds to
genuine resilience.

## Method

Compliance with the dual-sourcing policy is assessed at tier 1 only, by design.
This review therefore looked one level further up, at the material inputs of
each qualified pack assembler.

The review did not conduct new fieldwork. It used the cathode sourcing
positions already recorded in each supplier profile and in the sub-tier
dossiers produced by the 2026 mapping exercise. Those positions are not
reproduced here; the individual dossiers remain the record of what each
supplier buys and from whom.

## What the review can state

Two conditions have to hold for a dual-source arrangement to deliver
independence:

1. The two suppliers must not share a production site or a locality exposed to
   the same physical event.
2. The two suppliers must not depend on the same upstream producer for a
   material with no ready substitute.

Condition 1 is satisfied for the LI-18650. The two assembly plants are on
different continents.

Condition 2 cannot be evaluated from any single document in our records. It
requires taking the set of suppliers who supply this specific component,
retrieving each supplier upstream position, and testing the intersection. Our
ERP holds the first of those and none of the rest. Our document set holds the
rest and none of the first.

## Finding

The review records that Northwind currently has no system able to perform that
intersection, and that the answer for the LI-18650 is therefore unknown at the
board level even though every fact needed to derive it already exists inside
the company.

This is recorded as finding RR-2026-03-01 and is the origin of the deep-tier
graph proposal.
