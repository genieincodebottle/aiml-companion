# Supplier Audit Report: Prahara Polymers Pvt Ltd

Audit date: 20-21 January 2026
Site audited: Prahara Pune Moulding Works, Pune, India
Standard: ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 13485:2016 surveillance

## Summary

No nonconformances were raised. Two observations were recorded, both closed
before the report was issued.

## Fire suppression system

The audit gave particular attention to the fire suppression system, following
the 2024 industry alert on resin storage fires at moulding operations.

Prahara has installed a wet pipe sprinkler system throughout the moulding hall
and a separate clean-agent system in the raw material store, where the
polycarbonate and ABS resin is held. The clean-agent system was commissioned in
November 2025 and its acceptance test certificate was reviewed and found in
order.

Sprinkler head inspection records were complete for the preceding 12 months.
The resin store is separated from the moulding hall by a two-hour fire rated
wall, which exceeds the local code requirement of one hour. Emergency egress
routes were clear and marked.

Observation PRA-2026-01 (closed): the fire alarm panel log had not been
exported since commissioning. Prahara demonstrated the export function during
the audit and added a monthly export step to its maintenance schedule.

Observation PRA-2026-02 (closed): two of the eleven Northwind tools were stored
outside the designated tool rack. Relocated during the audit.

## Tooling

All eleven Northwind-owned tools were physically located and their asset tags
verified against our register. No discrepancies.

## Conclusion

Prahara remains an approved supplier with no open findings. This is the fourth
consecutive audit with no nonconformances raised.
