# Engineering Change Notice 2026-114

Part affected: PCB-A7 Main Controller Board
Revision: B to C
Products affected: NW-500 Patient Monitor, NW-220 Infusion Pump
Status: approved, effective from lot 2026-27

## Reason for change

The Revision B board used a power management IC that the semiconductor vendor
placed on last-time-buy notice in February 2026. Revision C replaces it with a
pin-compatible successor from the same vendor and adds two passive component
changes required by the successor datasheet.

## Impact assessment

Electrical: verified equivalent across the full operating envelope. No change
to any published product specification.

Regulatory: assessed as a like-for-like component substitution. No design
change requiring notification under the medical device regulation.

Supply: the change does not alter the board supplier, the laminate
specification or the sub-tier position. Meridian Circuits remains the
manufacturer and the SUB-CCL4 laminate grade is unchanged.

## Inventory disposition

Approximately 2,100 Revision B boards remain in stock and will be consumed
before Revision C is introduced. No scrap.
