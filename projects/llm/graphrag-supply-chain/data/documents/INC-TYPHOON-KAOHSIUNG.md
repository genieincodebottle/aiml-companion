# Supply Disruption Bulletin: Typhoon Meilin

Bulletin reference: SDB-2026-014
Status: ACTIVE
Issued by: Northwind Supply Risk

## Event

Typhoon Meilin made landfall on the southwest coast of Taiwan on 16 August
2026. Kaohsiung recorded sustained winds above 150 km/h and approximately
680 mm of rainfall over 40 hours.

## Reported impact in Kaohsiung

- The Port of Kaohsiung suspended container operations on 15 August. Partial
  resumption began 19 August with a backlog described by the port authority as
  three to four weeks.
- Industrial power supply in the southern industrial zones was interrupted for
  between 18 and 60 hours depending on feeder. Several zones remain on
  restricted supply.
- Local authorities have advised that manufacturing sites in the affected zones
  should plan for two to three weeks of degraded operation.

## Scope of this bulletin

This bulletin records the event and its effect on the city of Kaohsiung. It
does not attempt to enumerate which Northwind products are affected.

That assessment cannot be made from this document. It requires tracing every
Northwind supplier and sub-tier supplier located in Kaohsiung through to the
components they feed and the finished products those components appear in. The
supply risk team has been asked to produce that trace and it is not available
at the time of issue.

## Immediate actions

1. Confirm operating status with every supplier holding a Kaohsiung site.
2. Confirm operating status with known tier-2 suppliers in Kaohsiung.
3. Freeze non-essential engineering changes on affected part numbers.
4. Report finished-goods and work-in-progress cover to the weekly risk call.
