# Supplier Audit Report: Sentinel Optics Corp

Audit date: 17-19 February 2026
Site audited: Sentinel Kaohsiung Optics Plant, Kaohsiung, Taiwan
Standard: ISO 9001:2015 surveillance

## Summary

One major nonconformance raised and open. Sentinel technical capability
remains excellent; the finding concerns process discipline in the packaging
area rather than product design.

## Finding SEN-2026-01 (Major, OPEN)

Electrostatic discharge control in final packaging. Wrist strap continuity
testing in the final packaging cell was logged once per shift rather than per
operator per shift as the procedure requires. Two of six ionisers in the cell
were outside their verification interval. Surface resistivity measurements on
three packaging benches were above the 1E9 ohm procedural limit.

The SEN-IR12 read-out integrated circuit is ESD sensitive to Class 1B. A latent
ESD defect on this part would not necessarily be caught by end-of-line test and
could present as a field failure.

Corrective action requested: restore per-operator continuity logging, verify or
replace the ionisers, and remediate the bench surfaces. Sentinel committed to a
31 May 2026 completion. Status as of publication: OPEN.

## Finding SEN-2026-02 (Minor, CLOSED)

Germanium blank incoming records for one lot were missing the supplier
certificate. Certificate retrieved and filed during the audit.

## Business continuity

Sentinel was asked about typhoon preparedness given the Kaohsiung location.
Sentinel maintains a documented plan covering plant shutdown and restart, and
holds roughly three weeks of finished goods for its highest-volume parts.
Sentinel confirmed it holds no off-island inventory and no alternative
production site.
