# Sub-Tier Dossier: Formosa Substrate Materials

Formosa Substrate Materials ("Formosa Substrate", sometimes abbreviated FSM in
supplier correspondence) is a tier-2 supplier. Northwind Instruments has no
direct commercial relationship with Formosa Substrate Materials and no purchase
orders are placed with them. They appear in our risk register because of what
they supply to the tier above.

## What they make and who they supply

Formosa Substrate Materials produces copper-clad laminate. They supply the
high-Tg laminate grade designated SUB-CCL4 to Meridian Circuits, which uses it
as the base material for the boards it builds for us.

Formosa Substrate Materials also supplies laminate to several other electronics
manufacturers in the region. Northwind volume is a small fraction of their
output, which means Northwind has essentially no commercial leverage over their
allocation decisions during a shortage.

## Location

Formosa Substrate Materials operates a single laminate line at a facility in
Kaohsiung, Taiwan. The company has announced no plans for a second line.

## Upstream

Formosa Substrate Materials buys electrodeposited copper foil from Sarawak
Copper Foil, a producer in Kuching, Malaysia. This is a tier-3 relationship and
was identified during the 2026 deep-tier mapping exercise rather than through
any supplier disclosure.

## Why this dossier exists

Formosa Substrate Materials is invisible in our ERP. Nothing in our purchasing
system, our approved vendor list, or our supplier scorecards contains their
name. The 2026 deep-tier mapping exercise added them to the risk register after
tracing the laminate question through Meridian.
