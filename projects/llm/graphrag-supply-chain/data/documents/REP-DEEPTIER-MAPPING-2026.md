# Report: 2026 Deep Tier Mapping Exercise

Report reference: RR-2026-01
Owner: Head of Supply Risk

## Objective

To identify tier-2 suppliers behind every critical component, and tier-3
suppliers where the tier-2 position is a single source, per NWI-SC-009.

## Coverage achieved

All eleven critical components were mapped to tier 2. Five tier-2 suppliers
were identified that appear nowhere in Northwind purchasing systems. Two
tier-3 relationships were identified.

Three suppliers declined to name a sub-tier source. One of those declinations
concerns a display panel and is recorded as an open transparency gap.

## Principal observation

The exercise found that the information required to assess deep-tier risk was
already inside Northwind before it began. It was in supplier profiles, audit
notes, questionnaire responses and correspondence. What did not exist was any
way to ask a question across those documents.

The specific failure mode is worth stating precisely. A question such as which
finished products are exposed to a disruption at a given location cannot be
answered by searching our documents, because no document contains the answer.
Each document contains one link in a chain: a location, a site, a supplier
relationship, a component. The answer is the chain, and the chain has never
been written down anywhere.

## Recommendation

That the relationships identified by this exercise be held in a form that
supports traversal rather than search, and that the document set remain the
evidence of record behind each relationship so that any derived answer can be
traced back to the sentence it came from.
