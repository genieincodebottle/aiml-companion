# Supplier Profile: Kaigan Display Works

Kaigan Display Works ("Kaigan") supplies the DSP-3300 5.5 inch TFT display
module used in the NW-500 Patient Monitor. Kaigan has been qualified since 2016
and is currently our sole source for this part.

## Manufacturing footprint

Production is concentrated at the Kaigan Kaohsiung Fab in Kaohsiung, Taiwan.
Kaigan has no second display line; the Taichung site referenced in older
documentation was closed in 2023 and its tooling was moved to Kaohsiung.

## Sub-tier dependencies

Kaigan sources its chemically strengthened cover glass from Kaohsiung
Precision Glass, a specialist processor located in the same industrial zone as
the Kaigan fab. Kaigan cites the co-location as a lead-time advantage: cover
glass moves between the two facilities by truck in under an hour.

The TFT panel itself is purchased from a panel maker Kaigan declines to name
under its own confidentiality obligations. Northwind has flagged this as an
open transparency gap; see the 2026 supplier transparency review.

## Commercial

- Payment terms: net 45
- Contractual lead time: 84 days
- Minimum order quantity: 500 modules
- Quality system: ISO 9001 and ISO 13485, both current

## Assessment

Kaigan is technically excellent and commercially reasonable. The risk profile
is dominated by geography rather than by performance: the fab, its cover glass
processor, and the industrial zone they share are all in one city.
