# Sub-Tier Dossier: Baltic Lithium Salts

Baltic Lithium Salts is a tier-2 specialty chemicals producer based in Tallinn,
Estonia. Northwind has no direct relationship with them and places no orders.

## What they make

Baltic Lithium Salts produces NMC cathode powder for lithium-ion cell
manufacturers. The company operates one production facility, in Tallinn.

## Who they supply

Baltic Lithium Salts supplies NMC cathode powder to Volta Cell Systems. Baltic
Lithium Salts also supplies NMC cathode powder to Nordcell Energi AB.

Each of those relationships is documented in the respective supplier's own
profile and neither supplier presents it as unusual. Both selected Baltic
Lithium Salts independently and for different reasons: Volta on chemistry
qualification, Nordcell on Baltic Sea logistics.

## Capacity

Baltic Lithium Salts has publicly stated a nameplate capacity sufficient for
roughly 9 GWh of cell production per year and has an expansion under
construction with a stated 2028 completion. In a supply interruption their
customers would be allocated pro rata against contracted volume.

## Note for the risk register

This dossier records only what Baltic Lithium Salts makes and who they sell to.
The implication of those two customer relationships for Northwind's own
dual-sourcing position is assessed in the 2026 battery supply concentration
review, not here.
