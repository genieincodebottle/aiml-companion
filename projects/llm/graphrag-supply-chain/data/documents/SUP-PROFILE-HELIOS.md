# Supplier Profile: Helios Fluidics BV

Helios Fluidics BV ("Helios") manufactures the PMP-77 peristaltic pump head,
the fluid-delivery core of the NW-220 Infusion Pump. Two PMP-77 units are
fitted per pump. Helios is our sole source and has been qualified since 2015.

## Manufacturing footprint

Production is at Helios Eindhoven Works in Eindhoven, Netherlands. Helios has
no second production line for this part family.

## Sub-tier dependencies

The PMP-77 drive uses a neodymium-iron-boron permanent magnet rotor. Helios
purchases finished sintered NdFeB magnets from Anhui Rare Earth Refining in
Anhui, China. Helios has stated that no European or North American source is
qualified for the grade and tolerance the PMP-77 requires, and that qualifying
one would be a 12 month programme.

Stainless components, tubing interfaces and bearings are multi-sourced within
the EU.

## Commercial

- Payment terms: net 30
- Contractual lead time: 98 days, the longest in our base
- Quality system: ISO 13485 current, MDSAP certified

## Assessment

Helios is the longest-lead item we buy and sits on a rare-earth dependency with
a single non-EU source. The combination of a 98 day lead time and a 12 month
requalification programme means a Helios disruption is the slowest of all our
supply risks to recover from.
