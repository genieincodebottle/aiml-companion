# Supplier Profile: Meridian Circuits Sdn Bhd

Meridian Circuits Sdn Bhd (internally "Meridian") has been a qualified
Northwind Instruments supplier since 2014. Meridian manufactures both of our
printed circuit board assemblies: the PCB-A7 main controller board and the
PCB-B2 sensor interface board. Both are currently single-sourced to Meridian.

## Manufacturing footprint

All Northwind volume is produced at the Meridian Penang Plant in Penang,
Malaysia. Meridian operates a second facility in Johor, but it is not qualified
for medical-grade assemblies and carries no Northwind part numbers.

## Sub-tier dependencies

Meridian does not produce its own substrate. The copper-clad laminate used in
every Northwind board, internally designated SUB-CCL4, is purchased from
Formosa Substrate Materials. Meridian has confirmed in writing that Formosa
Substrate Materials is its only qualified laminate source for the high-Tg
grade our boards require, and that requalifying an alternative laminate would
take an estimated 20 to 26 weeks including IPC-6012 requalification.

Meridian's solder paste, connectors and passive components are multi-sourced
and are not considered a concentration risk.

## Commercial

- Payment terms: net 60
- Contractual lead time: 63 days for PCB-A7, 56 days for PCB-B2
- Capacity ceiling: approximately 6,500 boards per month combined
- Quality system: IATF 16949 and ISO 13485, both current

## Assessment

Meridian is a strong performer on quality and delivery. The concentration risk
is not Meridian itself but what sits behind it: two of our four finished
products cannot be built without a Meridian board, and Meridian cannot build a
board without laminate from a single upstream source.
