# Policy: Deep Tier Supply Chain Mapping

Policy reference: NWI-SC-009, revision 1
Owner: Head of Supply Risk

## Purpose

To define how far beyond our direct suppliers Northwind maps its supply chain,
and how the resulting information is maintained.

## Depth

Mapping is required to tier 2 for every component meeting the criticality test
below. Mapping to tier 3 is required only where the tier-2 position is a single
source.

The criticality test: a component is critical if it is single sourced, or has a
lead time over 60 days, or appears in two or more finished products.

## Method

Tier-2 information is obtained by structured supplier questionnaire, by
discussion during scheduled audits, and by open-source research. It is not
obtainable from our ERP, which by design records only what we buy and from
whom.

Suppliers are not contractually obliged to disclose their own sources and some
decline. A declined disclosure is recorded as a gap in the register, not as an
absence of risk.

## Maintenance

The map is reviewed annually and after any event that changes a supplier
footprint. Because the source material is unstructured, correspondence,
questionnaires, audit notes and public filings, the register is maintained as a
document set rather than as a table.

Revision 1 note: this policy was created after the 2026 mapping exercise
demonstrated that the information existed across our documents but could not be
queried, because nobody had ever connected it.
