# Policy: Supplier Qualification and Surveillance

Policy reference: NWI-SC-001, revision 11
Owner: Director of Supplier Quality

## Qualification

A new supplier is qualified through: a documented capability assessment, an
on-site audit against ISO 9001 as a minimum and ISO 13485 where the part is
used in a medical device, first-article approval, and a production part
approval at rate.

## Surveillance frequency

- Suppliers of components used in medical devices: on-site audit every 12 months
- All other production suppliers: on-site audit every 24 months
- Suppliers with an open major nonconformance: a follow-up audit within 6 months
  of the finding, regardless of the standard interval

## Finding classification

Major: a systemic failure of a required control, or any failure with a credible
route to product escape.
Minor: an isolated lapse with no credible route to product escape.
Observation: a practice that is compliant but carries avoidable risk.

A major finding does not by itself suspend a supplier. Suspension requires a
demonstrated product impact or a failure to progress corrective action.

## Sub-tier audit rights

Northwind contracts grant a right of access to sub-tier facilities where the
sub-tier supplies a critical characteristic. This right has been exercised
twice since 2019. Both exercises required more than three months of
negotiation, and neither was resisted on contractual grounds; the delay was
practical rather than legal.
