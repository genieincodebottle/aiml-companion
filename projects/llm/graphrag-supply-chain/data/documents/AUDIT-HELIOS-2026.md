# Supplier Audit Report: Helios Fluidics BV

Audit date: 8-9 April 2026
Site audited: Helios Eindhoven Works, Eindhoven, Netherlands
Standard: ISO 13485:2016 surveillance

## Summary

No nonconformances. One observation, closed.

Helios operates a mature quality system with strong design-control discipline
and complete device history records for the PMP-77.

## Observation HEL-2026-01 (closed)

Incoming inspection of NdFeB magnet lots verified dimensional characteristics
and surface finish but did not verify magnetic flux density, relying instead on
the supplier certificate. Given the single-source position on this material,
auditors recommended an independent verification step. Helios agreed and
introduced sample flux testing on 2 May 2026; evidence reviewed and observation
closed.

## Supply continuity discussion

Helios was asked to comment on its rare-earth magnet exposure. Helios confirmed
it holds approximately 14 weeks of magnet inventory, above its normal 8 week
policy, as a deliberate hedge against export licensing delay. Helios also
confirmed that this buffer was built during 2025 and that rebuilding it after a
drawdown would take roughly two quarters.
