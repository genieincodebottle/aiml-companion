# Sub-Tier Dossier: Kaohsiung Precision Glass

Kaohsiung Precision Glass is a tier-2 supplier with no direct Northwind
relationship. They process chemically strengthened cover glass and supply it to
Kaigan Display Works for use in display modules, including the DSP-3300.

## Location

Kaohsiung Precision Glass operates from a single site in Kaohsiung, Taiwan, in
the same industrial zone as the Kaigan Kaohsiung Fab. The two companies have a
truck-based delivery arrangement measured in hours rather than days, and neither
holds meaningful buffer stock of finished cover glass.

## Risk characteristics

Co-location is a cost and lead-time advantage in normal operation and a risk
multiplier in a regional disruption. A single event affecting the Kaohsiung
industrial area would stop the glass processor and the display fab
simultaneously, so Kaigan's ability to keep building from stock would be
limited by whatever finished glass happened to be on its own floor.

## Upstream

Kaohsiung Precision Glass buys raw float glass from two suppliers, one in
Taiwan and one in mainland China. This upstream leg is not considered a
concentration risk.
