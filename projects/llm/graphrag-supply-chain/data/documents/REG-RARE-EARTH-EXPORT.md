# Regulatory Notice: Rare Earth Magnet Export Licensing

Issued by: Northwind Regulatory Affairs

## Scope

Export licensing measures covering sintered neodymium-iron-boron (NdFeB)
permanent magnets and certain rare earth intermediate materials remain in force
in the principal producing jurisdiction. Licences are assessed per shipment and
per declared end use.

## Observed effects

Reported effects on comparable industrial buyers during 2025 and 2026:

- Licence processing adding four to ten weeks to shipment timelines.
- Requests for end-use documentation reaching two tiers beyond the applicant.
- Occasional refusals where end use could not be documented to the satisfaction
  of the licensing authority.

Medical device end use has not been identified as a category of concern in any
published guidance. No Northwind supplier holds written assurance to that
effect, and the absence of a stated concern is not an exemption.

## Applicability to Northwind

The measures apply to NdFeB magnets. Northwind does not purchase magnets
directly. Any exposure reaches us through a supplier that buys them, which
means the exposure cannot be assessed from our purchasing records and must be
traced through the supplier base.

## Action

Supply risk to maintain a current list of Northwind components whose production
depends on NdFeB magnets at any tier, and the finished products those
components appear in.
