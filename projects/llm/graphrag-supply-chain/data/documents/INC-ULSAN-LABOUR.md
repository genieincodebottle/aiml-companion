# Supply Disruption Bulletin: Ulsan Industrial Action

Bulletin reference: SDB-2026-009
Status: CLOSED

## Event

A four-day partial strike affected several manufacturing employers in Ulsan,
South Korea between 2 and 5 June 2026 as part of a regional wage dispute.

## Impact

The Volta Ulsan Cell Plant operated at reduced staffing for three of the four
days. Volta reported a shortfall of approximately 11 days of Northwind pack
output, recovered by 28 June through overtime.

No Northwind line stopped. Nordcell was asked whether it could cover a longer
interruption and confirmed it could add roughly 15 percent to its current
Northwind allocation within six weeks, but not more, because of its stated
capacity ceiling.

## Note

This event tested the dual-source arrangement on the LI-18650 for the first
time since Nordcell was qualified in 2022. It tested capacity. It did not test
whether the two suppliers share upstream exposure, because the disruption was
a labour event local to one plant.
