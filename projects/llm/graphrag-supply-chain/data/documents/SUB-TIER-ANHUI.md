# Sub-Tier Dossier: Anhui Rare Earth Refining

Anhui Rare Earth Refining is a tier-2 producer of sintered neodymium-iron-boron
permanent magnets, located in Anhui, China. Northwind has no direct commercial
relationship with them.

## Who they supply

Anhui Rare Earth Refining supplies finished NdFeB magnets to Helios Fluidics,
which fits them in the rotor assembly of the PMP-77 peristaltic pump head.

## Regulatory exposure

Sintered NdFeB magnets have been subject to export licensing measures in
several jurisdictions since 2025. Licence processing has been reported to add
four to ten weeks to shipment timelines with no advance notice, and licences
have on occasion been declined for end uses the exporter could not fully
document. Medical device end use has not to date been a category of concern,
but Helios has confirmed it holds no written assurance to that effect.

## Alternatives

Non-Chinese sintered NdFeB capacity exists in Japan, Germany and increasingly
in the United States, but at the grade and dimensional tolerance the PMP-77
rotor requires the qualified pool is small and heavily booked. Helios estimates
a 12 month qualification programme for any alternative.
