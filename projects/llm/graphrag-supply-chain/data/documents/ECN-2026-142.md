# Engineering Change Notice 2026-142

Part affected: SEN-PM25 Particulate Sensor
Products affected: AQ-100 Air Quality Sensor
Status: approved

## Reason for change

To increase the Aeronova Sensing share of SEN-PM25 volume from 40 percent to
50 percent, and to validate that Aeronova can hold a larger allocation ahead of
any need to move further.

## Rationale

The AQ-100 is our highest unit-volume product. Its sensor is currently weighted
towards a supplier whose plant shares a city with the display fab used by our
highest-revenue product. Rebalancing towards the German site reduces the number
of Northwind products whose supply is concentrated in one locality.

This is a deliberate geographic decision rather than a quality or cost one.
Both suppliers meet specification and the unit costs are within four percent of
each other.

## Validation

Aeronova produced three qualification lots at the higher rate between April and
June 2026. All three passed. Aeronova confirmed no change to its own upstream
arrangements is required at this volume.

## Note

This ECN addresses one component. The wider question of how many Northwind
products depend on a single locality across all tiers is not in scope here and
is held by the supply risk team.
