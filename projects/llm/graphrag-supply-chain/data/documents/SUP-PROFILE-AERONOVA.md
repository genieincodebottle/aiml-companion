# Supplier Profile: Aeronova Sensing GmbH

Aeronova Sensing GmbH ("Aeronova") was qualified in 2021 as the second source
for the SEN-PM25 particulate sensor and currently carries about 40 percent of
Northwind volume on that part.

## Manufacturing footprint

Aeronova produces at the Aeronova Dresden Plant in Dresden, Germany.

## Sub-tier dependencies

Aeronova's laser diode and photodiode pair are purchased from two qualified
optoelectronics vendors, one in Germany and one in Japan. Aeronova's moulded
optical path is produced in house. No single upstream dependency has been
identified in the qualification file or in the 2026 audit.

## Commercial

- Payment terms: net 30
- Contractual lead time: 42 days
- Capacity: Aeronova has stated it can absorb 100 percent of Northwind SEN-PM25
  demand within one quarter if required

## Assessment

Aeronova is the healthiest dual-source arrangement in the base: a different
country, a different continent from the primary, a different upstream pool, and
enough capacity to take the whole part. It is the model the sourcing team
should be measured against, not the exception.
