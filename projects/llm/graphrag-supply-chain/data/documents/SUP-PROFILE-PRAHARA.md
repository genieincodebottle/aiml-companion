# Supplier Profile: Prahara Polymers Pvt Ltd

Prahara Polymers Pvt Ltd ("Prahara") is our sole source for all four polymer
enclosures: ENC-500, ENC-220, ENC-TX9 and ENC-AQ. Prahara has supplied
Northwind since 2011 and holds the injection moulding tooling for every one of
these parts.

## Manufacturing footprint

All Northwind parts are moulded at the Prahara Pune Moulding Works in Pune,
India. Prahara operates a second plant in Aurangabad that produces automotive
trim only and holds no Northwind tooling.

## Tooling ownership

The tools are owned by Northwind Instruments and held at Prahara under a bailment
clause. In a supplier transition the tools can be recalled, but the practical
transfer time is 10 to 14 weeks including trial shots and first-article
approval. Tooling ownership limits commercial exposure; it does not eliminate
lead time exposure.

## Sub-tier dependencies

Prahara compounds its own polycarbonate/ABS blend from resin purchased on the
open market across at least four Indian and Southeast Asian distributors.
Prahara's resin supply is genuinely diversified and is not a concentration
risk. Colourants and flame retardant masterbatch are also multi-sourced.

## Commercial

- Payment terms: net 45
- Contractual lead time: 28 to 42 days depending on part
- Quality system: ISO 9001 current, ISO 13485 current

## Assessment

Prahara is the clearest example in our base of single-sourcing that is low risk
despite looking alarming on a spreadsheet. Four sole-sourced parts sit with one
vendor, but the tooling is ours, the material is a commodity, and the sub-tier
is diversified. Compare with Meridian, where a single sole-source line item
carries a hidden upstream monopoly.
