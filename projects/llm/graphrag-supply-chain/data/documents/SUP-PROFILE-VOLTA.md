# Supplier Profile: Volta Cell Systems

Volta Cell Systems ("Volta") is the primary supplier of the LI-18650 battery
pack, carrying approximately 70 percent of Northwind volume. Nordcell Energi AB
carries the remaining 30 percent as our qualified second source. The LI-18650
appears in three of our four finished products.

## Manufacturing footprint

Cells are produced and packs assembled at the Volta Ulsan Cell Plant in Ulsan,
South Korea.

## Sub-tier dependencies

Volta purchases its NMC cathode powder from Baltic Lithium Salts, a
specialty chemicals producer based in Tallinn, Estonia. Volta has held this
relationship since 2019 and describes Baltic Lithium Salts as its only
qualified cathode source for the cell chemistry used in the LI-18650.

Anode graphite and separator film are multi-sourced across three vendors each
and are not treated as concentration risks.

## Commercial

- Payment terms: net 60
- Contractual lead time: 70 days
- Quality system: ISO 9001, IATF 16949, current
- Transport: sea freight standard, air freight available at premium

## Assessment

Volta performs well. Northwind's dual-sourcing policy is formally satisfied on
this component because a qualified second source exists at Nordcell. Whether
that second source delivers genuine independence is a question this profile
cannot answer on its own, and it is examined in the 2026 battery supply
concentration review.
