# Supply Disruption Bulletin: Penang Monsoon Flooding

Bulletin reference: SDB-2025-021
Status: CLOSED (retained for reference)

## Event

Sustained monsoon rainfall caused localised flooding across parts of Penang,
Malaysia in late November 2025. Two industrial estates recorded standing water
and road access was restricted for approximately 60 hours.

## Impact

The Meridian Penang Plant was not flooded. Meridian reported that road access
for inbound and outbound freight was restricted for two and a half days and
that approximately 900 boards shipped four days late. No Northwind line stopped
as a result, because finished-goods cover on both board part numbers exceeded
three weeks at the time.

## Lessons recorded at closure

The event was absorbed entirely by inventory cover. The risk team noted at the
time that the same event occurring during a low-cover period would have stopped
two product lines within a week, and that cover on Meridian parts should not be
allowed to fall below two weeks without an explicit decision.

That recommendation was accepted and is tracked in the inventory policy review.
