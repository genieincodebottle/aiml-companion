# Policy: Dual Sourcing Requirements

Policy reference: NWI-SC-004, revision 6
Owner: Director of Supply Chain

## Purpose

To define when a purchased component must have more than one qualified source.

## Requirement

A component must have at least two qualified sources when any of the following
is true:

- The component represents more than 8 percent of the cost of goods sold of any
  finished product it appears in; or
- The component appears in two or more finished products; or
- The component has a contractual lead time exceeding 60 days.

A component meeting none of these may be single sourced with the written
approval of the Director of Supply Chain, recorded in the sourcing file.

## What counts as a qualified second source

A second source is qualified only when it has completed first-article approval,
passed a supplier audit within the preceding 24 months, and demonstrated
production of at least one full lot at rate.

Capacity is not part of the qualification test. A qualified second source that
cannot absorb full volume still satisfies this policy. Whether it satisfies the
business is a separate judgement and is recorded in the risk register, not
here.

## Sub-tier

This policy applies to direct suppliers only. It sets no requirement on the
sourcing arrangements of our suppliers.

Revision 6 added this sentence in response to the 2026 deep-tier mapping
exercise, which found that the policy had been informally assumed to cover
upstream independence. It does not, and never has.

## Exceptions register

Current approved single-source exceptions are recorded in the sourcing file and
reviewed annually at the January supply board.
