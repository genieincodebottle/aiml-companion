# Review: Supplier Transparency Gaps

Review reference: RR-2026-02

## Purpose

To record where a supplier has declined, or been unable, to disclose
information Northwind considers necessary for risk assessment.

## Open gaps

**Kaigan Display Works, TFT panel source.** Kaigan declines to name the panel
maker behind the DSP-3300, citing its own confidentiality obligations. Kaigan
has offered to confirm in writing that the panel maker holds ISO 9001 and has
no open regulatory action, without naming it. Supply risk has accepted this as
a partial mitigation. The gap remains open.

**Volta Cell Systems, cathode requalification timeline.** Volta names its
cathode source but declines to discuss what requalifying an alternative would
take, describing it as commercially sensitive. The consequence is that we can
identify the dependency but cannot estimate recovery time from it.

**Sarawak Copper Foil, upstream cathode supply.** Not a declination. Nobody
asked. Tier-4 is outside the scope of NWI-SC-009 and remains unmapped.

## Closed during 2026

**Helios Fluidics, magnet source.** Helios named Anhui Rare Earth Refining in
response to the 2026 questionnaire, having declined in 2024. Gap closed.

## Note on interpretation

A transparency gap is not a risk finding. It is an absence of evidence. Several
of the gaps above may sit behind perfectly diversified supply. The register
exists so that absence of evidence is never quietly recorded as evidence of
absence.
