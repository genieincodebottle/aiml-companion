# Supplier Audit Report: Meridian Circuits Sdn Bhd

Audit date: 10-12 March 2026
Site audited: Meridian Penang Plant, Penang, Malaysia
Standard: ISO 13485:2016 supplier surveillance

## Summary

Meridian Circuits continues to meet Northwind requirements on process control,
traceability and change management. One major nonconformance and two minor
observations were raised. The major finding is open at the time of publication.

## Finding MER-2026-01 (Major, OPEN)

Moisture-sensitive device handling. Floor-life tracking for MSL-3 and MSL-4
components was maintained on a shared spreadsheet rather than in the MES.
Auditors identified two reels of BGA packages whose recorded exposure time
exceeded the manufacturer floor life, and which had been placed back into
active stock without a documented bake cycle.

Neither reel was traced to a Northwind board, and no Northwind product is
believed to be affected. The finding is raised as major because the control is
systemic rather than because a specific escape occurred.

Corrective action requested: move floor-life tracking into the MES with an
automated block on expired reels. Meridian committed to a 30 June 2026
completion date. Status as of publication: OPEN, on schedule.

## Finding MER-2026-02 (Minor, CLOSED)

Calibration records for two reflow oven thermocouples were 11 days past due.
Recalibrated during the audit, records corrected, closed on site.

## Finding MER-2026-03 (Minor, CLOSED)

The incoming inspection procedure referenced a superseded revision of the
laminate acceptance specification. Document corrected, closed on site.

## Sub-tier observations

Meridian confirmed again that laminate supply is single sourced and that no
alternative has been qualified. Auditors noted that this has been recorded as
an accepted risk in three consecutive audits without a mitigation plan being
opened. This is recorded as an observation rather than a finding, because
sub-tier sourcing is a Northwind commercial decision and not a Meridian
quality-system defect.
