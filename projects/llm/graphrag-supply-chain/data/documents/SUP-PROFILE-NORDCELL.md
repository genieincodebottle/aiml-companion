# Supplier Profile: Nordcell Energi AB

Nordcell Energi AB ("Nordcell") was qualified in 2022 as the second source for
the LI-18650 battery pack, following the dual-sourcing mandate issued after the
2021 cell shortage. Nordcell currently carries about 30 percent of Northwind
volume on this part.

## Manufacturing footprint

Nordcell produces at the Nordcell Skelleftea Plant in Skelleftea, Sweden. The
plant is powered substantially by hydroelectric generation, which is why the
Nordcell pack carries a materially lower embodied carbon figure than the Volta
equivalent. Sustainability reporting uses the Nordcell figure where the pack
origin is known.

## Sub-tier dependencies

Nordcell buys NMC cathode powder from Baltic Lithium Salts in Tallinn,
Estonia. Nordcell selected this supplier partly for logistics: Tallinn is a
short Baltic Sea crossing from the Swedish plants, and the shipping lane is
active year round.

Nordcell manufactures its own cell casings and sources separator film from two
European converters.

## Commercial

- Payment terms: net 30
- Contractual lead time: 56 days, shorter than Volta
- Capacity ceiling: currently 40 percent of Northwind annual LI-18650 demand,
  so Nordcell cannot absorb full volume without an 18 month expansion

## Assessment

Nordcell is a capable supplier with a shorter lead time than the incumbent. Its
capacity ceiling means it cannot be treated as a drop-in replacement for Volta
at full volume.
