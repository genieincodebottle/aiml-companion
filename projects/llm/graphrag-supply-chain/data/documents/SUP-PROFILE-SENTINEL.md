# Supplier Profile: Sentinel Optics Corp

Sentinel Optics Corp ("Sentinel") supplies two sensing components: the SEN-IR12
infrared sensor array used in the TX-9 Thermal Imager, and the SEN-PM25
particulate sensor used in the AQ-100 Air Quality Sensor. SEN-IR12 is sole
sourced. SEN-PM25 is dual sourced, with Aeronova Sensing GmbH carrying the
balance.

## Manufacturing footprint

Both parts are produced at the Sentinel Kaohsiung Optics Plant in Kaohsiung,
Taiwan.

## Sub-tier dependencies

Sentinel operates an unusually integrated process. Germanium optics are ground
and coated in house, the read-out integrated circuit is bought from a
multi-sourced foundry pool, and packaging is done on site. Sentinel's declared
sub-tier concentration is limited to germanium blank supply, which it sources
from two vendors in Belgium and China.

## Commercial

- Payment terms: net 60
- Contractual lead time: 112 days for SEN-IR12, 49 days for SEN-PM25
- Quality system: ISO 9001 current

## Assessment

Sentinel is well diversified upstream. Its risk to Northwind is almost entirely
locational: the plant is in Kaohsiung, the same city as the Kaigan display fab,
so a regional event hits two nominally unrelated parts of our bill of materials
at once.
