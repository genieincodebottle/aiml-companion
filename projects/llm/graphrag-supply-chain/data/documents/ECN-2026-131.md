# Engineering Change Notice 2026-131

Part affected: ENC-500 Polymer Enclosure
Products affected: NW-500 Patient Monitor
Status: approved, effective from lot 2026-31

## Reason for change

The flame retardant masterbatch used in the ENC-500 blend is being replaced
with a halogen-free equivalent, in line with the Northwind restricted
substances roadmap. The change is initiated by Northwind, not by the supplier.

## Impact assessment

Mechanical: impact strength and heat deflection retested on moulded samples.
Both within specification. Wall section unchanged, tooling unchanged.

Flammability: UL 94 V-0 rating retested and retained at the same wall
thickness.

Supply: Prahara Polymers remains the moulder. Prahara sources masterbatch from
multiple distributors and has confirmed the halogen-free grade is available
from at least three of them, so the change does not narrow the sub-tier
position.

Colour: a slight shift in the off-white reference was accepted by industrial
design. New colour standard issued.

## Inventory disposition

Existing ENC-500 stock, approximately 3,400 units, remains fully compliant and
will be consumed first.
