# Supply Disruption Bulletin: Helios Eindhoven Compressor Fire

Bulletin reference: SDB-2026-007
Status: CLOSED

## Event

A compressor unit in the plant room at Helios Eindhoven Works overheated and
caught fire on the evening of 28 April 2026. The clean-agent system activated
and the fire was contained to the plant room. There were no injuries.

## Impact

Production was suspended for 36 hours pending an insurer inspection and a
compressed-air system restart. Helios reported no damage to production
equipment, tooling or finished goods.

Approximately 400 PMP-77 units shipped two days late. Northwind absorbed the
delay from safety stock with no effect on NW-220 build schedules.

## Observation

The event is closed with no corrective action requested of Helios. It is
retained in the register because it is the only production interruption at the
Eindhoven site in the last five years, and because the 98 day lead time on this
part means even a short interruption is worth recording.
