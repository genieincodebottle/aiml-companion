# Sub-Tier Dossier: Sarawak Copper Foil

Sarawak Copper Foil is a tier-3 supplier identified during the 2026 deep-tier
mapping exercise. They produce electrodeposited copper foil at a facility in
Kuching, Malaysia.

## Who they supply

Sarawak Copper Foil supplies electrodeposited copper foil to Formosa Substrate
Materials, which uses it in copper-clad laminate production.

## Why a tier-3 supplier is in the register at all

Three degrees of separation from Northwind is normally past the point of useful
visibility, and most of our tier-3 relationships are deliberately not tracked.
This one is recorded because the chain beneath it is unusually narrow: a single
foil producer feeding a single laminate line feeding a single board supplier
feeding two of our four finished products.

The mapping exercise did not attempt to go beyond tier 3. Sarawak Copper Foil's
own copper cathode supply is unmapped and should be assumed to be diversified
in the absence of evidence, not assumed to be safe.
