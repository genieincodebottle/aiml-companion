# Supplier Audit Report: Volta Cell Systems

Audit date: 6-8 May 2026
Site audited: Volta Ulsan Cell Plant, Ulsan, South Korea
Standard: IATF 16949 and ISO 9001 surveillance

## Summary

No major nonconformances. Two minor findings, both closed before publication.

## Finding VOL-2026-01 (Minor, CLOSED)

Cell formation data retention was set to 24 months against the contractual
requirement of 60 months for medical end use. Retention policy amended and
historic data confirmed recoverable from archive. Closed 30 May 2026.

## Finding VOL-2026-02 (Minor, CLOSED)

Two of the twelve pack assembly torque drivers had verification labels that
were legible but past their stated interval by four and six days respectively.
Reverified on site.

## Sub-tier

Volta was asked to confirm its cathode powder sourcing position. Volta restated
that it holds a single qualified cathode source and declined to discuss
alternative qualification timelines, describing them as commercially sensitive.
Auditors recorded this as a transparency limitation rather than a finding.
